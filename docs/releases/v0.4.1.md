Billing and a full back office for `--Auth saas`, and shadcn/ui with dark mode on every page.

## Changes to know about

- **With accounts, the admin page is a back office with sections.** Users are at `/admin` with
  `--Auth identity` and at `/admin/users` with `saas` (where `/admin` is the dashboard). The
  user menu in the header is a dropdown.
- **The UI uses [shadcn/ui](https://ui.shadcn.com/)** (MIT) and the theme's colours, and dark
  mode follows the operating system. New client dependencies: `radix-ui`, `lucide-react`,
  `class-variance-authority`, `cn`, `tw-animate-css`, `shadcn` (for its Tailwind styles) and the
  Geist font, served by the app itself.
- **EF Core:** new migrations add the audit log (`identity` and `saas`), and billing and back
  office tables (`saas`).

Projects created from earlier versions don't change: a template update only affects new projects.

## New

- **Plans, trials and limits** (`--Auth saas`):
  - A plan catalogue in one place (`Billing/Plans.cs`), with prices per currency, features and
    limits. Plans have versions, so customers keep their price when a plan changes.
  - A reverse trial: a new organisation gets the top plan for 14 days, then the free plan.
  - One check for features and limits (`Entitlements`), in Wolverine `Before` methods (402
    Payment Required) and in the client (`GET /api/billing`). Features are feature flags
    ([Microsoft.FeatureManagement](https://github.com/microsoft/FeatureManagement-Dotnet)).
  - Limits on what exists (open Todos, members including invitations) and on use per billing
    period (reminders a month, counted in one SQL statement so requests at the same moment
    can't pass it), with a warning at 80%.
  - A downgrade never deletes data: nothing more can be added over a limit, and paid features
    switch off.
  - A public, pre-rendered pricing page, generated from the catalogue.
- **Stripe subscriptions** (`--Auth saas`; [Stripe.net](https://github.com/stripe/stripe-dotnet),
  Apache-2.0):
  - Stripe Checkout (keeping what's left of the trial) and the customer portal.
  - Signed webhooks, stored once and handled from a durable Wolverine queue, keep a local copy of
    each organisation's billing state and invoices.
  - `dotnet run -- stripe-prices` creates the catalogue's prices in Stripe.
  - Deleting an organisation cancels its subscription.
  - Payments sit behind `IPaymentProvider`, so another provider can replace Stripe.
  - Without Stripe keys, the app runs with the trial and the free plan only.
- **Back office** (`/admin`):
  - Users (with accounts): ending a user's sessions and resending the confirmation email, on
    top of roles, locks and invitations.
  - An audit log of every admin action.
  - Failed messages: Wolverine's dead-letter queue, to replay or discard.
  - With `saas`:
    - A dashboard: monthly recurring revenue, organisations, trials ending, failed payments.
    - Organisations: members, plan, usage, invoices (with Stripe links), and giving a plan
      until a date (or extending a trial), suspending, notes and deleting.
    - Feature flags for everyone, a plan, or one organisation.
- **shadcn/ui** on every page, with shared `Section` and `Messages` components, and dark mode
  with no script, so pre-rendered pages show the right colours at once.
